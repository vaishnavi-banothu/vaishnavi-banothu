Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • NeedBridge Platform
1. Personal Data Collection & Principles

NeedBridge collects only essential information required to facilitate community support: your name, email address, general city/neighborhood location, and listing details. We never sell, rent, or publicly display private residential street addresses or private phone numbers without consent.

2. Location Privacy Safeguards

To safeguard user privacy and safety, all interactive discovery maps and listing cards display only generalized neighborhood coordinates (e.g. Madhapur or Kondapur public area centers). Exact doorstep locations are never revealed publicly.

3. Direct Messaging Privacy

Direct messages exchanged between users are strictly restricted to the conversation participants. Conversations are encrypted in transit and accessible only to participants and platform moderators when a content violation report is lodged.

4. Safeguards for Minors

Users under 18 years of age are encouraged to participate in community donations, study circles, and tutoring under adult guardian supervision. Minors should never meet in secluded or private locations for community exchanges.

Community Standards & Acceptable Use Policy
1. Health and Care Resources Disclaimer

Important Healthcare Notice:

NeedBridge provides listings for clinics, blood donation contacts, and community health camps for public awareness purposes only. NeedBridge does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment recommendations, or doctor consultations. In case of medical emergencies, always call official emergency medical services (such as 108 or 112) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.

2. Community Code of Conduct

NeedBridge is built on mutual respect and neighborly solidarity. The following conduct is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate account suspension:

  • Posting commercial spam, illicit drugs, prohibited weapons, or misleading services.
  • Fraudulent pricing, bait-and-switch donation schemes, or extortion.
  • Harassment, abusive communication, or discriminatory behavior.
  • Attempting to bypass security controls or access administrative interfaces without authorization.
3. Academic Demonstration Scope

This platform is developed as a computer science project for educational, community demonstration, and research purposes.
